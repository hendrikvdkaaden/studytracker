Privacy Policy for Deadly

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Deadly mobile application ("Deadly", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Deadly is a study planning app that helps you track deadlines, plan study sessions and monitor your progress.

We have designed Deadly to be an offline-first app. The overwhelming majority of your data never leaves your device.

Summary

  • Your study data stays on your device. We cannot see it.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not have a user account system, so we never ask for your email or password.
  • The only data that leaves your device is the information required to process and validate a subscription purchase.

Data stored on your device

The following information is created by you and stored locally on your device using an on-device database. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Goals and deadlines (title, subject, date, type, difficulty, target study time, completion status)
  • Planned and completed study sessions (date, time, duration, notes)
  • Subjects you create
  • Your name or nickname and school name, if you choose to enter them
  • App preferences such as theme choice and reminder timing

This data is removed when you delete the app from your device. Because it is stored only on your device, we are unable to recover it for you if it is lost.

Subscription data

Deadly offers an optional auto-renewing premium subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription management provider.

When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives and processes:

  • A randomly generated, app-specific user identifier
  • Your purchase and subscription status (which product was bought, when it renews or expires)
  • Basic device and country information supplied by the app store

This information is used solely to validate your purchase and to unlock premium features on your device. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. This identifier is not linked to your name or email address.

Apple processes the payment itself. We never receive or have access to your payment card details. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Deadly schedules reminders for your study sessions and deadlines. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to us or through any external server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Data we do not collect

Deadly does not collect, use or share:

  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, calendar or health data
  • Your name, email address or any other contact information

Deadly does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children's privacy

Deadly is intended for general audiences and is suitable for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because study data never leaves the device and no account is required, the app does not gather personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Your rights and choices

Because your study data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Access and correction: all your data is directly viewable and editable within the app.
  • Deletion: deleting an item in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all local data.
  • Subscription: you can view, manage and cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID Account Settings.

If you would like your subscription-related records removed from RevenueCat, contact us at the address below and we will arrange it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated within the app.
